# Estimate & budget
Open a project → **Estimate**.

1. Choose a tier: **Basic, Standard or Premium**. Facilities for that tier are pre-ticked from your Facility master; unit costs come from the master for the project's area type (you can edit them).
2. Enter quantities. Add stage / approval costs and other costs.
3. vector7 shows total production cost, sellable area (total × sellable %), cost per sellable sq ft, **MRP per sq ft = cost per sq ft × MRP multiplier**, and the estimated duration from stage durations and dependencies (critical path, working days).
4. Export the estimate as **PDF** or **Excel**.

Saving the estimate moves the project to **Go / No-Go**.
