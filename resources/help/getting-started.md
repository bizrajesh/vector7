# Getting started
Welcome to your vector7 promoter workspace. A typical first week:

1. **Finish the setup wizard** — organisation profile, then upload your pre-configuration template or choose *Use App defaults*.
2. **Add your team** in **Users & roles** (Manager, Sales, Account, Support). Generate a password or email a set-password link.
3. **Check Settings** — booking validity, sale completion window, instalment plan, disclaimers, ID prefixes and **Listings & leads** (default contact number, contact modes, lead auto-assignment).
4. **Put something on the marketplace**:
   - an already-approved layout → **Projects → Quick launch layout**;
   - a new layout you are still getting approved → **Projects → New project** (estimate, Go/No-Go, tracking, launch);
   - a single flat, house, villa, plot, farm land or shop → **Property listings → New listing**.
5. **Work your leads** — every call, WhatsApp, enquiry, chatbot hand-off and booking appears in **Leads**.

Use the **assistant** (chat button in the top bar) to ask “how do I …” or questions about your data.
