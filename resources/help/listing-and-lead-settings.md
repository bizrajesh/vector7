# Listings & leads settings
Settings → **Listings & leads**:

- **Default listing contact** — name, mobile, WhatsApp and email pre-filled on new listings and quick-launch layouts, and which modes buyers may use (Call, WhatsApp, Email).
- **Lead auto-assignment** — new leads are given to active Sales users in turn (round-robin). Off = leads stay unassigned until someone takes them.
- **Lead stages** — rename, reorder or add stages. New, Won and Lost always exist.
- **WhatsApp templates** — quick messages for the lead page; use {name}, {listing}, {user} and {promoter}.
- **Photo watermark** — a small vector7 mark on uploaded photos.
- **Workspace assistant** — switch the AI chat on or off for your workspace.
