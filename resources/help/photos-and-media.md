# Photos, brochure & video
Every listing and layout can have **3 to 20 photos**, a **brochure PDF** (max 10 MB) and a **YouTube link**.

- **Upload:** drop photos on the box or tap to choose. JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each.
- **What happens:** each photo is resized to 1600 px wide with a 400 px thumbnail, converted to WebP and stripped of EXIF location data. A small *vector7* watermark is added if it is switched on in Settings → Listings & leads.
- **Order & cover:** drag photos to reorder (or use the ← → buttons) and press **Make cover**.
- **Captions:** type and press ✓.
- Photos count toward your plan's storage.
