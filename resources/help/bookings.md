# Bookings
**New booking:** Bookings → **New booking** → choose the project and an available plot, then an existing customer or register a new one (they get an email to set a password). Enter a promo code if any, tick the booking disclaimer and save.

- Only one customer can book a plot; a booked plot cannot be booked again.
- The booking is valid for the booking validity period in **working days** (Saturdays, Sundays and your holidays are skipped).
- If the 1st instalment is not paid in time, the plot is released automatically and the customer and Sales Team are emailed. A reminder goes out one working day before.
- The offer price is used if the offer is active on the booking date; the prices are stored on the booking.
- Every booking also creates or updates the buyer's **lead** and marks it Won.
