# Dashboard
The dashboard changes with your role:

- **Admin** — projects by status, budget vs actual, plot inventory, sales value, collections and overdue instalments.
- **Manager** — stage progress, delayed sub-tasks and budget alerts.
- **Sales** — bookings expiring, instalments due or overdue, new leads and follow-ups, conversion.
- **Account** — collections today and this month, expenses, receivables.
- **Support** — open leads and help-desk tickets.

The **bell** shows alerts such as plan usage above 80 %, bookings expiring within a day, overdue instalments and lead follow-ups due today.
