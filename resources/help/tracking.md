# Project tracking
Open a project → **Tracking**.

- Update each sub-task's status (Not started, In progress, Blocked, Done), actual dates, actual cost and notes.
- Upload the documents required by the checklist. A sub-task can only be **Done** when its mandatory documents are uploaded and the tasks it depends on are Done.
- Stage progress and project progress update automatically.
- When spend crosses the budget alert % (Settings), the Management and Accounts groups get an email.
- When every approval stage is done, press **Mark Ready to Launch**.
