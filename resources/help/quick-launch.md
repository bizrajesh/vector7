# Quick launch a layout
**Quick launch** is for promoters whose layout is already approved and who only want to sell.

**Who:** Tenant Admin and Manager. Sales and Support can view launched layouts but cannot launch.

**Steps** (Projects → **Quick launch layout**):
1. Layout name, approval type, approval authority and number (DTCP / BDO / CMDA / RERA — optional, shown as a trust badge), survey and patta numbers, total extent.
2. Location — village, town, district, state, PIN and a **map pin** (tap the map).
3. The approved **layout picture**, **3–20 site photos**, an optional YouTube link and brochure PDF.
4. **Features** (tick chips) and up to 10 highlights of your own.
5. **Promotion text** — or tap **Write with AI**.
6. **Contact details** and the modes buyers may use (Call, WhatsApp, Email).
7. **Plots** — upload the CSV / Excel file or paste the CSV. Same columns as the normal launch import; download the sample from the page.

Press **Launch layout**. If any plot row has an error, nothing is saved and the rows to fix are listed.

The layout is labelled **Direct launch**, has no Estimate or Tracking tabs, counts toward your plan's project limit, and works for booking, sales, payments, registration, accounts and reports like any other project. To start tracking approvals later, open the project and choose **Convert to full project** — plots and bookings are kept.
