# Marketing & SEO
**Marketing** writes posts for launched projects with AI and publishes them on schedule to connected Instagram / Facebook accounts, with a metrics dashboard. **SEO** holds default titles, per-page meta (locked when edited), the AI-crawler decision for robots.txt, and **Rebuild now** for meta and the sitemap — including published listings and category pages.
