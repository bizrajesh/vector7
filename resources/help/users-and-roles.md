# Users & roles
- **Add a user** — choose the role and either **Generate password** (shown once with Copy; optionally emailed; ask to change at next login) or email a set-password link.
- **Generate password** for any user later, or for several at once (one-time Excel).
- **Unlock** users locked after 5 wrong passwords.
- **Roles** — create custom roles by combining existing permissions.
- **Notification groups** — tag users to groups such as Management, Sales Team and Accounts; emails go to groups.
- **Bulk import** users from CSV (full_name, email, mobile, role, notification_groups).
Plan limits apply to the number of users per role.
