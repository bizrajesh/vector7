# Accounts
- **Receipts** — booking and sale payments (income).
- **Expenses** — add project expenses with category, stage or facility, vendor and bill upload.
- **Budget vs actual** per project, stage and facility.
- **Day book** and **Receivables** (dues and overdue instalments). Everything exports to Excel.
