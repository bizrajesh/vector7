# Services, enquiries & help desk
- **Services** — what vector7 offers to promoters and buyers; requests arrive as tickets.
- **Enquiries** — general enquiries to vector7. Assign one to a promoter and it becomes a **lead** in their workspace.
- **Help desk** — tickets with category, priority, SLA, assignee, internal notes and attachments.
