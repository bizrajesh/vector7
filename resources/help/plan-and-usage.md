# My plan & usage
Settings → **My plan & usage** shows each limit with **Limit / Used / Left over / % used**: storage, projects, active listings, images per listing, AI credits this month and users per role. You get a banner and an email at 80 % and 100 %, and 7 days and 1 day before the plan ends. Press **Upgrade** to change plan.
