# My plan & usage
Settings → **My plan & usage** shows each limit with **Limit / Used / Left over / % used**: storage, projects, plots, users (all roles), active listings, images per listing and AI credits this month. You get a banner and an email at 80 % and 100 %, and 7 days and 1 day before the plan ends.

- **Upgrade or renew:** choose Starter, Growth or Enterprise.
- **Top-ups:** need a little more without changing plan? Buy extra users, projects, plots, storage or listings. Prices are per month + GST. A top-up lasts until your plan period ends, so you pay for the months left in it. It shows as *incl. +… top-up* in the usage table once it is paid.
- You always keep **5 active listings free**, even if your subscription lapses.
