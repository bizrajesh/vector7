# Plans
vector7 ships with three plans — **Starter**, **Growth** and **Enterprise** — and you can edit them or add more.

Every plan is designed on five **plan items**:

| Item | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Storage | Files, photos and documents (MB) |
| Projects | Open projects and layouts |
| Plots | All plots across the tenant's layouts |
| Users (all roles) | Everyone who can sign in to the workspace |
| Active listings | Listings live on the marketplace |

Use **−1** for unlimited. **Other limits** are images per listing and AI credits per month. **Optional caps per role** (for example "1 Tenant Admin") stay at −1 unless you need them; the *Users (all roles)* limit already counts everyone.

Every tenant keeps the **free active listings** (default 5) even when a plan's number is lower or the subscription has lapsed. Change that number, and the largest photo size (default 5 MB), under **Contact view charges**.

Tenants can go beyond their plan with **top-ups** — see *Top-up packs*.
