# Sales & payments
**Start a sale:** on an Available plot, or on a Booked plot for the same customer. The first payment must be at least the 1st instalment (e.g. 30 %). The plot moves to **Sale Init**.

**Record a payment:** open the sale → **Record payment** — cash, cheque, bank transfer or UPI, reference number and proof. Previous payments for the plot are shown. A numbered **receipt PDF** is emailed to the customer.

Instalments are tracked with due dates in working days. When nothing is due the plot becomes **ROR** (ready for registration).

Customers do not pay online in this release — all payments are recorded by your team.
