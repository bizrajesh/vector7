# Leads
**Leads** collects everyone interested in your properties: enquiry forms, **Call** and **WhatsApp** clicks, **Show number**, chatbot hand-offs, AI-search contacts and bookings — plus walk-ins and phone calls you add yourself.

**Pipeline:** New → Contacted → Site visit scheduled → Site visited → Negotiation → **Won** (booked / sold) or **Lost** (a reason is required). Stages can be renamed in Settings → Listings & leads.

**Board and list:** drag cards between stages on the **Board** (or use *Move to* on each card), or switch to **List** to filter by stage, source, listing, assignee, follow-up due today / overdue and dates. Export to Excel.

**Lead page:**
- **Call** (opens the dialler and logs the call), **WhatsApp** (pick a template; the message is logged), **Email**, **Log / note**.
- **Schedule** a follow-up or site visit — reminders arrive at 9:00 and 15 minutes before.
- **Book plot** converts the lead to a booking with the buyer pre-filled; the lead becomes Won.
- **AI help:** summarise the lead, suggest the next step, or draft a WhatsApp / email follow-up in English or Tamil.

**Who sees what:** Admin and Manager see all leads; Sales see leads assigned to them and unassigned ones; Support can work leads but not delete them.

**Duplicates:** a contact with the same mobile or email is merged into the existing lead and the new interest is added to its timeline.

**Auto-assign:** switch on round-robin among Sales users in Settings → Listings & leads.
