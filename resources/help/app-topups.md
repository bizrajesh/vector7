# Top-up packs
A top-up pack adds a number of units to one plan item — **users, projects, plots, storage or active listings** — for a monthly price (excl. GST).

- **Add a pack:** choose what it adds to, a name tenants will see (e.g. "5 GB extra storage"), the units (storage in MB: 5120 = 5 GB) and the price per month.
- **On sale** decides whether tenants can buy it. A pack that has been bought is switched off instead of deleted.
- **How tenants pay:** on *My plan & usage* they pick a pack and a quantity. The top-up runs until their current plan period ends, so the invoice is *price × quantity × months left* (rounded up) + GST. With a gateway it opens the payment page; without one, mark the invoice paid on the tenant's page.
- **Free top-ups:** on *Tenants & usage → a tenant*, use **Grant a free top-up** (e.g. a launch offer). You can cancel any top-up there.

The **Latest top-ups** table lists the most recent purchases and grants.
