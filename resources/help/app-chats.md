# Chatbot conversations
Marketplace and workspace assistant conversations are kept for **90 days**. App Admin can filter, read and export them to Excel.

## AI search log
**AI search log** (button at the top of Chatbot conversations) lists every plain-words search from the marketplace: what the buyer typed, how it was understood (the filter chips), what was relaxed when there were fewer than 5 matches, and how many results came back. Filter **No results only** to see what buyers want that nobody lists yet — useful for telling promoters what to list. Export to Excel with the buttons at the top. Searches are deleted after 90 days.
