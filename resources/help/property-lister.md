# Property lister workspace
**Property listers** are owners and agents who only post and track listings. They are separate from layout promoters. They sign up and sign in from the marketplace (**Sign in → Property lister**, **Post property**, or **List your property** in the footer).

**What you see**
- **Dashboard** — live listings, drafts, views, enquiries, open call-back requests, and how many buyers paid to see your number. Each listing shows its own views, enquiries and unlocks.
- **My listings / Post a property** — the same listing editor promoters use: photos, video, map pin, price, offers and contact options. Publish to go live, mark Under offer or Sold / Rented, or unpublish.
- **Enquiries & call-backs** — every buyer who called, messaged, asked for a call back (with their preferred mode and time) or unlocked your number. Each comes with their contact details and a follow-up reminder.
- **My plan & usage** — Lister Basic / Plus / Pro, or any plan the vector7 team creates for listers. You can buy top-ups for extra listings or storage.

**Free listings:** you always keep 5 active listings free, even after a plan ends.
