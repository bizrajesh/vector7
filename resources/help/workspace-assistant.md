# Workspace assistant
The **assistant** (chat button in the top bar) answers:
- **How-to questions** — from these help pages, with a link to the right screen.
- **Questions about your data** — e.g. “How many plots are available in Sunrise Nagar?”, “Show overdue instalments this week”, “Which leads need follow-up today?”, “Total expenses on Green Valley this month”. It only reads data your role is allowed to see.
- **Drafting** — listing descriptions, promotion text, social posts, follow-up messages and email replies. Copy the draft or **Apply** it to the field you last clicked.

It never changes data on its own. The only actions are *Apply draft* and *Create follow-up* on a lead, each after you press **Confirm**. Each question uses your workspace's AI credits; when they run out you'll see an Upgrade button. A Tenant Admin can switch the assistant off in Settings → Listings & leads.
