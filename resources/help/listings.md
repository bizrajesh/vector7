# Property listings
Post single properties that are not part of a layout: **Individual plot, Flat / Apartment, Farm land, Commercial space, Villa, Independent house**.

**Who:** Tenant Admin and Manager create, edit, publish, unpublish and delete. Sales and Support can view listings and work their leads.

**Create:** Property listings → **New listing** → choose the type. Fill the basics, the type's details (BHK, areas, facing, extent, water source…), location and map pin, price (and an optional offer with its end date), patta / survey number (required for plots and farm land), features and contact. The listing is saved as a **draft**.

**Photos:** after saving, upload 3–20 photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each). Drag to reorder, pick a cover and add captions. Photos are resized, converted to WebP and stripped of location data. **Check photos with AI** warns about screenshots, phone numbers or other sites' watermarks.

**Publish** needs at least 3 photos, a price, the location and one contact mode. Published listings appear on the marketplace immediately and count toward your plan's active-listing limit.

**Statuses:** Draft, Published, Under offer (still visible), Sold / Rented (removed from the marketplace), Unpublished.

Buyers cannot book listings online — they call, WhatsApp or enquire, and each contact becomes a **lead**. Record a sale in **Accounts** if you want.
