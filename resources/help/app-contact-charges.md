# Contact view charges
Buyers browse every listing for free. To see an owner's or promoter's **phone number** they pay a small fee. It goes to the **App owner**, not the tenant, and the same prices apply to every tenant.

- **Listings (default)** and **Layouts** — prices in ₹, GST included. Set 0 to keep that kind free.
- **Price by listing type** — e.g. charge less for rentals or farm land. Leave blank to use the default.
- **Unlock lasts** — how many days one payment keeps that property's number visible to the buyer (0 = no end).
- The charge only applies when an online gateway (Razorpay or Swipe) is set up in **Settings → Payments**. Without one, numbers stay free.
- **Request a call back** is always free. The buyer leaves name, mobile, how to contact them (call, WhatsApp, email) and the best time, and it becomes a lead in the tenant's **Leads & enquiries**.

**Contact view payments** lists every payment with receipt number, buyer, property, owner and amount, plus revenue and GST totals for the dates you pick. Use **Excel** to export.

The same screen sets the **free active listings** every tenant keeps (default 5) and the **largest photo** size (default 5 MB).
