# Tenants & usage
One row per tenant with plan, status, dates, days left and a bar per limit (teal under 80 %, amber 80–99 %, red at 100 %). Open a tenant for usage history and its largest files, change the subscription, suspend or reactivate, and mark invoices paid when no gateway is configured.
