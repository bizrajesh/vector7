# Settings
- **Organisation** — logo, address, contacts and social links (used on the marketplace, emails and PDFs).
- **Booking, sales & refunds** — booking validity and sale completion window (working days), instalment plan (must total 100 %), refund penalties and app settings (sellable %, broker commission, budget alert %, MRP multiplier).
- **Disclaimers** — booking, sale, registration and refund text shown and accepted at each step.
- **Listings & leads** — default listing contact and contact modes, lead auto-assignment, lead stages, WhatsApp templates, photo watermark and the workspace assistant switch.
- **ID seeds** — prefixes for project, user, booking, sale, expense, income, listing and lead IDs.
- **Working days** — holidays skipped when counting validity and due dates.
- **My plan & usage** — limits, used and left over, and Upgrade.
